# Iris as a governing layer

Iris is not a second chatbot beside the model. Iris is the layer the model has to speak through.

A certified Iris does four things, in this order:

1. **Ask the question.** Was a human member of the team able to personally review the specific facts of this specific situation?
2. **Record one finding.** SOVEREIGN, NULL, or AMBIGUOUS. Process language is not a person.
3. **Bind the model.** If a finding already exists, the model may explain it. The model may not change it, soften it, or grade the principle.
4. **Stay attributable.** The reply names the model and says Iris governed it. Iris is not legal advice.

Federation peers are optional. A deployment with zero peers is not a broken Iris. The gate is on either way. Chat models, when configured, are counted separately from peers.
