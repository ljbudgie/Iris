# Iris as a governing layer

Iris is not a second chatbot beside the model. Iris is the layer the model has to speak through.

A certified Iris does four things, in this order:

1. **Ask the question.** Was a human member of the team able to personally review the specific facts of this specific situation?
2. **Record one finding.** SOVEREIGN, NULL, or AMBIGUOUS. Process language is not a person. Iris computes this in [`lib/ai/governing-layer.ts`](../lib/ai/governing-layer.ts) before the model is called.
3. **Bind the model.** The finding is injected as a binding note. The model may explain it. The model may not change it, soften it, or grade the principle. A letter Iris asks the model to draft must contain the question word for word.
4. **Stay attributable.** The reply names the model and says Iris governed it. The finding chip on screen is the gate's finding, not the model's opinion.

Ordinary conversation is not classified. A person's own description of what happened to them is not treated as the institution's reply. A pasted reply, or a message in the institution's voice, is.

Federation peers are optional. A deployment with zero peers is not a broken Iris. The gate is on either way. Chat models, when configured, are counted separately from peers.
