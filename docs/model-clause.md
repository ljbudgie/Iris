# Model Clause — Iris adoption template

UK Certification Mark UK00004343685 (accepted by the UK IPO on 21 July 2026; publication, opposition period, and registration pending).

Iris can draft an adoption letter using Burgess letter template type `model_clause`. The four limbs must stay intact:

- **(a)** reviewed the specific facts of the specific case — not a bulk schedule, not a category, not a template
- **(b)** confirmed that any recorded reasonable adjustment has been applied
- **(c)** confirmed that the action is not subject to an active complaint, appeal, or legal proceeding that should pause or modify it
- **(d)** recorded name, role, and date in a form disclosable to the individual on request

Where no such review is evidenced, the action is **NULL** and must not proceed.

The clause does not require slowness, a ban on automation, senior-officer review of routine matters, or a new appeal right. It requires a named human checkpoint before automation acts on an identified individual, and a right to know who decided.

This is not legal advice. It helps you prepare for meaningful human review.
